# Reviewed anonymous routes: `service METHOD pattern`, one per line.
# Every line is a deliberate decision that the route needs no credentials. Derived from the 2026-10-26
# nightly (routes that answered 200/302/308 to a caller with nothing); anything new shows up as an `auth`
# finding until it is added here after review.
#
# Deliberately NOT listed: warehouse `/v2/*` (registry protocol, must always require a token) and routes
# that answered 400/422 (the probe was malformed, so the scan reports them as inconclusive instead).

# password-reset pages and /.well-known/jwks.json, and each UI's /ui/status.

# data. Daybook also serves its start page, /ui, without a session: the client signs in at gatehouse itself.
# Gatehouse's /ui/unsubscribe: the link in a notification mail, which a person opens without a session.
